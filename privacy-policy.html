Privacy Policy – Word Search Challenge

InfinityAppsHub ("we", "us", or "our") built the Word Search Challenge mobile application (Android Package ID: com.wordquest.wordquest). We respect your privacy and designed Word Search Challenge as an offline-first word puzzle game that stores your gameplay progress locally on your device without requiring account registration or login.

No Login Required Local On-Device Storage Google AdMob Disclosed DPDP Act & Google Play Compliant
01

Overview and Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, stored, or processed when you install and play Word Search Challenge, how that information is used, and the choices you have regarding your data.

By installing or using Word Search Challenge, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy applies strictly to the Word Search Challenge Android mobile application published by InfinityAppsHub.

Word Search Challenge does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or physical location to play.
02

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All core gameplay and progression data is created and stored strictly locally on your device inside the Application's private SQLite database (wordquest_v1.db) and Android SharedPreferences:

  • Game Progress & Statistics: Completed levels across the 50 puzzle categories (25 levels per category = 1,250 curated puzzles across Easy 8×8, Medium 10×10, Hard 12×12, and Expert 15×15 grids), star ratings (1–3 stars), found words, active/paused board snapshots, elapsed puzzle timers, and lifetime scores.
  • Virtual Coin Wallet & Ledger: Starting welcome coins (250 coins), coins earned from level completions (25–50 coins) and Daily Challenge completions (30–100 coins), coins spent on word hints (50 coins), and coins spent on individual level or category unlocks (2,000 coins). Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value.
  • Daily Challenge History: Calendar dates of completed Daily Challenges and your 7-day streak progress.
  • App Preferences: Your toggles for Sound Effects, Vibration & Haptics, and 9:00 AM Daily Challenge notification reminders.
We do not operate a proprietary remote server to harvest or view your local SQLite gameplay database.
03

Information We Do NOT Collect

InfinityAppsHub does not ask for, collect, or store any of the following personal or sensitive user data:

  • Personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you voluntarily email our support team).
  • Precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, photos, media files, camera, or microphone recordings.
  • Financial, payment, banking, or credit card details.
04

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Search Challenge is free to play and is supported by advertisements served via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). To display banner ads, frequency-capped interstitial ads between completed levels, and optional user-initiated rewarded video ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction data:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: The Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app-set ID used for frequency capping, ad delivery, fraud prevention, and (where permitted by your device settings) personalized or non-personalized advertising.
  • Coarse Device & Network Information: Device make, model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language setting, mobile carrier, and IP address (which may be used by Google to estimate general country/region location).
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad impressions, clicks, rewarded video completions, and SDK crash/performance diagnostics.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device's Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
05

Device Permissions Explained

Word Search Challenge requests only the minimum Android system permissions necessary for its core features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load advertisements when an internet connection is available. Core puzzle gameplay remains playable offline.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Requested only after showing an in-app explanation so the Application can send one optional local reminder at 9:00 AM for the Daily Challenge. You can deny this permission or turn reminders off anytime in the in-app Settings tab.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Allows the local 9:00 AM Daily Challenge alarm to reschedule automatically after you restart your phone, only if notifications are enabled.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play Services permission used by Google AdMob to access the user-resettable Advertising ID.
06

Local Notifications

Daily Challenge reminders are scheduled and triggered 100% locally on your Android device using Android's AlarmManager and NotificationManager. No external push notification servers (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging) track your device token.

You have full control over notifications and can enable or disable "Daily Challenge Reminders" at any time in the in-app Settings screen or through Android System Settings.

07

Data Retention, Backup and Deletion

Because your gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you have direct control over its retention and deletion:

  • How to Delete Your Data: You can permanently erase all locally stored game progress, coin balances, and preferences at any time by clearing the Application's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Word Search Challenge → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the Application.
  • Android Auto Backup: Depending on your device settings, Android's built-in Google Drive backup may automatically back up and restore your app data across reinstalls on devices signed into your Google account. You can manage or delete app backups in your Google Drive / Android System Backup settings.
08

Children's Privacy

Word Search Challenge is a general-audience word search puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not provide chat features, user-generated content, social profiles, or personal data collection forms.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has sent personal information to us via a support email, please contact us and we will promptly delete such correspondence.

09

Data Security

Your local game database and preferences are stored within Android's sandboxed application storage directory, which is protected by the Android operating system from access by other unauthorized apps. Any network communication performed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

You have the right to withdraw consent for optional permissions (such as notifications or the Android Advertising ID) at any time via your device settings and to erase your local application data.
